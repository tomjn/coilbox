armsolar.json: geometry of Beyond All Reason's Armada solar collector.

Model by Cremuss, credited as `model_author` in Beyond All Reason's own
units/ArmBuildings/LandEconomy/armsolar.lua.

Source: https://github.com/beyond-all-reason/Beyond-All-Reason
        objects3d/Units/armsolar.s3o

Changes made: converted from s3o to JSON, the piece tree flattened into one
mesh in model space, and UVs and texture references dropped. Vertex positions
are unchanged, in elmos, because the object exists to show the unit's real
size. No vertex was moved, added or removed.

armsolar.json is therefore an adaptation and is offered under the same licence,
CC BY-SA 4.0. That covers this file and the geometry in it, not the rest of
coilbox, which stays under the licence in LICENSE at the repository root.

Reproduce it with, from a checkout of Beyond All Reason:

  bun run lego:reference --s3o <BAR>/objects3d/Units/armsolar.s3o \
    --footprint 5 \
    --source "https://github.com/beyond-all-reason/Beyond-All-Reason, objects3d/Units/armsolar.s3o" \
    --author "Cremuss" \
    --licence "CC-BY-SA-4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/"

footprintSteps is 5, read from footprintx and footprintz in
units/ArmBuildings/LandEconomy/armsolar.lua, and confirmed by that unitdef's
5 by 5 yardmap.
