# Player guides

Guides for people who play games through coilbox. To install it, see [Install](../index.md#install) on the home page.

## Play

- [Singleplayer battles](singleplayer.md). Set up a battle against AI opponents, start it, and carry on from a saved game.
- [Sign in and chat](lobby-and-chat.md). Create an account on a lobby server, log in to one or more servers, and chat in channels and direct messages.
- [Multiplayer battles](multiplayer.md). Find and join a battle, host one on a lobby server or straight from your computer, and play with other people.
- [Campaigns and scenarios](campaigns-and-scenarios.md). Play a campaign one mission at a time, play a scenario on its own, and import ones somebody shared.
- [Replays](replays.md). Find a replay, watch it, read its players, chart, build orders, map and chat, and analyse it.
- [Career and player stats](career-and-stats.md). See your progress in each game, your record and achievements, and your record against another player.
- [Battle presets](presets.md). Save a battle setup, from singleplayer, a multiplayer room, a conquest or a Warpath fight, and play it again.
- [Galactic conquest](conquest.md). Take a map one battle at a time, on a galaxy of stars, a theatre chart, cities or provinces.
- [Warpath](warpath.md). Cross a map once, unlocking units and perks as you go, until you beat the boss or run out of health.
- [Replay rewind](replay-rewind.md). Watch a replay in the engine and seek, play backward and change speed, and what recording costs in time and disk.

## Library

- [Maps, games and units](library.md). Look through the maps and games you have installed, see how a map is played, read and compare a game's units, and find a game or map that is not listed.
- [Base blueprints](blueprints.md). Draw a layout of buildings, place it in a match with the in-game widget, save a base you built, and share blueprints on the hub.

## Set up

- [Get games, maps and engines](downloads.md). Download maps, map packs, games and engines, install a file you already have, choose the folders coilbox reads and saves to, and free up space.
- [Portable mode](portable-mode.md). Run coilbox from one folder, next to skylobby or another lobby, without touching your other settings.

## Share

- [Community hub](https://coilbox-hub.vercel.app). Browse presets, challenges, scenarios and blueprints other players have shared, and import them.
