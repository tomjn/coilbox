# Player guides

Guides for people who play games through coilbox. To install it, see [Install](../index.md#install) on the home page.

## Play

- [Battle presets](presets.md). Save a battle setup, from singleplayer, a multiplayer room, a conquest or a Warpath fight, and play it again.
- [Galactic conquest](conquest.md). Take a map one battle at a time, on a galaxy of stars, a theatre chart, cities or provinces.
- [Warpath](warpath.md). Cross a map once, unlocking units and perks as you go, until you beat the boss or run out of health.
- [Replay rewind](replay-rewind.md). Watch a replay in the engine and seek, play backward and change speed, and what recording costs in time and disk.

## Set up

- [Portable mode](portable-mode.md). Run coilbox from one folder, next to skylobby or another lobby, without touching your other settings.

## Share

- [Community hub](https://coilbox-hub.vercel.app). Browse presets, challenges, scenarios and blueprints other players have shared, and import them.
